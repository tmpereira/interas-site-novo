To celebrate **Women's Month**, Brazil's Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (MCTI) launched the webinar series **"Women who enable the future"** ("Mulheres que habilitam o futuro"). We are honored to announce that **Dr. Denise Zezell**, coordinator of INTERAS (INCT for Radiation in Health), is one of the researchers invited to this strategic series of conversations.

The initiative, organized by the General Coordination of Enabling Technologies (CGTH/MCTI), seeks to value women's leadership in Science, Technology and Innovation (ST&I) projects, strengthening science communication and inspiring new generations of women and girls to pursue careers in technological and scientific fields.

### Technologies that Transform
Throughout March, MCTI's official channel will publish weekly videos with researchers who lead projects in enabling technologies — fundamental areas that underpin innovation in health, energy and industry. In these conversations, they share not only their academic paths and achievements, but also the challenges they have overcome and the importance of diversity for the country's scientific development.

### Premiere and Paths of Impact
The series opened on March 3 with an enriching conversation with Prof. Dr. Solange Binotto Fagan (UFN), vice-coordinator of the INCT for Carbon Nanomaterials and coordinator of the **National Network of Women in Nanoscience**. **Dr. Denise Zezell**'s participation in the series reinforces the key role of **INTERAS** in the national landscape of health technologies and the strength of women's leadership in our National Institutes of Science and Technology (INCTs). **It will be published on March 17, 2026.**

### Follow the Series
We invite the entire INTERAS community to support this initiative and follow the episodes. The talks are an excellent opportunity to go behind the scenes of cutting-edge science done in Brazil by extraordinary women.
