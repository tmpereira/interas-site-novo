The National Institute of Science and Technology for Radiation in Health (**INCT INTERAS**) has just launched a trilogy of thematic institutional videos, designed to deepen, detail and complement the panoramic view presented in its [General Institutional Video](/videos/video-institucional-1/). The new audiovisual series covers the three fundamental scientific strands that structure the Institute's research and innovation: **Diagnosis**, **Translational Research** and **Treatment**.

Headquartered at the Instituto de Pesquisas Energéticas e Nucleares (**IPEN-CNEN**) and funded by the National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (**CNPq**), the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (**MCTI**) and the São Paulo Research Foundation (**FAPESP**), INTERAS brings together a large multidisciplinary network of partner institutions across Brazil — including the Universidade de São Paulo (**USP**), Universidade Federal de São Paulo (**UNIFESP**), Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro (**UFRJ**), Universidade Federal do ABC (**UFABC**), Universidade Federal de Sergipe (**UFS**), Universidade do Estado do Amazonas (**UEA**) and Universidade Estadual Paulista (**UNESP**).

The three new productions aim to bring the scientific community, health professionals and society closer to the solutions developed at the frontier of knowledge between radiation physics, photonics, nanotechnology and biomedicine.

---

### Diagnosis Axis: Spectroscopic Precision and Non-Invasive Liquid Biopsy

The first video in the series takes us into the **Diagnosis** laboratories, presenting state-of-the-art optical and spectroscopic methodologies dedicated to the early, non-invasive identification of serious diseases:

* **FTIR (Fourier Transform Infrared) Spectroscopy:** Using the molecular and vibrational signature of biofluids — with emphasis on the analysis of human saliva — to detect specific biomarkers without the need for invasive procedures.
* **Hyperspectral Imaging and Oral Cancer:** Applying advanced optical systems and artificial intelligence to map oral tissues in real time, allowing the detection of premalignant changes and tumors at early stages, which substantially improves clinical prognosis and the chances of cure.
* **Social and Clinical Impact:** Less discomfort for the patient, faster diagnosis and decentralized screening of suspicious lesions in the public health system.

### Translational Research Axis: From the Molecular Bench to Functional Evaluation

The second video addresses the role of **Translational Research**, the crucial link between fundamental laboratory discoveries and the validation of tools for biomedical and plant applications:

* **Green Nanotechnology with Femtosecond Lasers:** Synthesis of colloidal gold nanoparticles by ultrashort pulsed laser ablation, ensuring maximum chemical purity, colloidal stability and freedom from the toxic residues common to traditional chemical methods.
* **Radiosensitization:** Study of the mechanism by which these metallic nanoparticles enhance the ionizing radiation dose in the cellular microenvironment, multiplying the biological damage to cancer cells while preserving adjacent tissues.
* **Molecular Imaging in Plant Models with Micro-PET/CT:** Innovative use of the radiotracer ¹⁸F-FDG (fluorodeoxyglucose) and positron emission microtomography coupled with X-rays to monitor the metabolic dynamics and physiological responses of plants to environmental stresses, expanding the horizons of molecular imaging beyond the traditional biomedical model.

### Treatment Axis: Photonic Therapies, Nanocarriers and Radiation-Matter Interaction

Closing the trilogy, the third video presents the cutting-edge therapeutic fronts developed by INTERAS for precise and minimally invasive medical intervention:

* **Targeted Nanocarriers for Prostate Cancer:** Collaborative inter-institutional projects dedicated to developing functionalized gold-based carriers for the targeted delivery of therapeutic agents and radiopharmaceuticals directly into the prostate tumor, mitigating systemic adverse effects.
* **Nd:YAG Lasers and Nanoparticle Photoabsorbers:** Using near-infrared laser beams combined with photoabsorbing agents in dental procedures and soft tissues, allowing deep disinfection, bacterial decontamination and controlled tissue remodeling with high photothermal precision.
* **Preservation of Healthy Tissue:** Optimizing irradiation regimes and dosimetry to maximize the local curative effect, shorten recovery times and offer less debilitating treatment options.
