# 9th NanoMat: Where the Invisible Shapes the Future (and INTERAS is part of this story!)

How do you build tomorrow? For scientists in nanosciences and advanced materials, the answer is as fascinating as it is challenging: **one atom at a time**.

It is with this frontier vision that **INTERAS** is immensely proud to announce that we are co-organizers of the **9th edition of NanoMat**, the main scientific event of the Graduate Program in Nanosciences and Advanced Materials (PPG-NMA) at the Universidade Federal do ABC (UFABC). The conference will take place on **May 6 and 7, 2026**, at UFABC's Santo André Campus.

### Celebrating the Highest Excellence

If academia had a "World Cup", UFABC's PPG-NMA would have just lifted the trophy. The 9th NanoMat is not just a conference; it is a great celebration of a historic milestone: achieving the **top grade of 7 in the CAPES evaluation**.

To the general public this may seem like just a number, but in the world of science, a grade of 7 is the highest seal of approval. It attests that the research carried out there not only leads the national scene, but competes on equal terms with the best universities on the planet. It is recognition that Brazilian science produces innovation, technology and knowledge of international excellence. And INTERAS stands side by side with the program on this path of success.

### Architects of the Invisible: What's on at the event?

Imagine nanotechnology as a set of building blocks like *Lego*, but where each little block is infinitely smaller than the diameter of a human hair. When we manipulate matter at this *nanoscopic* scale, the rules of the game change, and ordinary materials gain "superpowers" — becoming stronger, more flexible or able to conduct electricity in surprising ways.

Under the theme of reflecting on the past, present and future of science, the ninth edition of NanoMat will bring together a constellation of renowned researchers to discuss how these atomic "superpowers" will solve real problems in our society. The program is not to be missed and features leading names in science on topics such as:

* Prof. Gustavo Dalpian (USP): Creation and History of PPG-NMA
* Prof. Paula Homem-de-Mello (UFABC Rector-elect): Strategies for strengthening graduate studies at UFABC
* Prof. Charles Morphy (Dean of Graduate Studies – UFABC): Recent history of graduate studies at UFABC and the CAPES Global project
* Prof. Marcelo A. Moret: Interdisciplinarity and the Challenges of the Field at CAPES
* Prof. Carlos Frederico de O. Graeff (Fapesp): Science, Technology and Innovation in the State of São Paulo: the role of Fapesp
* Prof. Juliana Bernardes (CNPEM / LNNano): Infrastructure and multi-user facilities at LNNano
* Prof. Derval Rosa dos Santos (UFABC): The Silent Water Crisis: Innovative Solutions for Emerging Contaminants
* Dr. Nicolas Ariel Garcia (Universidad Nacional der Sur – Argentina): Molecular Dynamics investigations in disordered and phase separated systems
* Prof. Marcelo Peres (UNIFEI): Detection of Dirac fermions in SnTe films using magnetotransport and microwave absorption measurements
* Prof. Rogéria Rocha Gonçalves (USP / Fapesp): Advances in Lanthanide Luminescence: From Materials Design to Biophotonic Applications
* Prof. Demétrio Jackson dos Santos (UFABC Vice-Rector-elect): Advanced Materials and Innovation at the Embrapii CTIM Unit

In addition to the talks, the event will have a special session dedicated to the winners of the CAPES Thesis Award, Dr. Ariany Bonadio and Dr. Gabriel Ravanhani Schleder, inspiring the new generation of scientists.

### A Space to Connect and Innovate

Science is not done in isolation behind locked laboratory doors; it flourishes through the exchange of ideas. NanoMat is designed precisely for this. Over the two days, students, postdocs and researchers will present their work, in oral communications or poster sessions. It is the perfect opportunity to network, get to know cutting-edge multi-user facilities and start collaborations that may lead to the country's next major technological advances.

### Take Part!

Whether you are an experienced researcher, an undergraduate or graduate student, or simply an innovation enthusiast: the 9th NanoMat is the place for you. Come celebrate CAPES grade 7 with us and get a glimpse of the technologies that will redefine our tomorrow.

To see the full program and register, visit the [official event page on the Doity platform](https://doity.com.br/ix-nanomat) or learn more on the [Nano UFABC](https://nano.ufabc.edu.br/nanomat/) website.
