# 2nd Workshop of the INCT for Radiation in Health: Science and Collaboration at Instituto D'Or

National scientific progress depends, fundamentally, on coordination and the exchange of knowledge among major research centers. On May 25, 2026, research on ionizing radiation will reach an important milestone with the **2nd Workshop of the INCT for Radiation in Health**.

This annual meeting, a key step in fulfilling the goals and targets of the National Institutes of Science and Technology (INCTs), will be held at the Instituto D'Or de Pesquisa e Ensino (IDOR), in the city of São Paulo. The event is not just an administrative formality, but a strategic moment to consolidate collaboration networks among major institutions such as IPEN (Instituto de Pesquisas Energéticas e Nucleares), UFABC, UFRJ, USP and other partner organizations.

### The Importance of the Annual Meeting

The INCTs' annual meetings are vital for monitoring scientific output and aligning research activities. The workshop allows researchers, students and collaborators to discuss the results obtained, present technological advances and debate future challenges in the application of ionizing radiation to human health. The presence of CNPq as funder reaffirms the project's standard of excellence and the need to report on its impact on the scientific community and on society.

### Program Highlights

The workshop will be a full day of activities, starting at 8:30 a.m. with the official opening and the presentation of progress reports by coordinators Denise Zezell and Emiliano Medei.

The event was designed to maximize interaction among participants:

* **Oral Sessions:** The schedule includes a series of 15-minute presentations (12 minutes of talk and 3 minutes for discussion), giving a panoramic view of the diversity of research carried out under the institute's umbrella.
* **Poster Sessions:** Two dedicated slots (10:15 to 10:45 a.m. and 3:45 to 4:30 p.m.) will be reserved for poster presentations, an essential space for students and young researchers to present their data and receive qualified *feedback* from experts in the field.

The program will include presentations by 23 INTERAS researchers, briefly showing the results, challenges and opportunities of their work and research groups. The day will close with a session dedicated to joint actions, aimed at strengthening institutional partnerships for the next work cycle.
