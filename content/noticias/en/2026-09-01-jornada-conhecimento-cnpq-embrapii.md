The National Institute of Science and Technology for Radiation in Health (**INCT INTERAS**) was one of the 30 National Institutes of Science and Technology selected in a public call by the National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (**CNPq**) to join the **Knowledge Journey** ("Jornada do Conhecimento") mentoring program, developed in strategic partnership with the Brazilian Company for Research and Industrial Innovation (**EMBRAPII**).

The initiative, launched in August 2026, was designed to bring the excellent science produced within the INCTs closer to technological innovation mechanisms and to the real demands of the national productive sector.

> ### About the CNPq-EMBRAPII Partnership
> The program aims to strengthen the technology transfer capacity and operational maturity of research groups, combining the experience of the Embrapii Units and Competence Centers with the frontier expertise of the INCTs, with a special focus on overcoming the challenges between bench research and industrial application (technology readiness levels TRL 4 to 7).

### The Initiative and the Operating Model

The selection process conducted by CNPq included 30 INCTs across the country. To enable a collaborative and in-depth way of working, the participating institutes were divided into 10 thematic groups, each made up of 3 INCTs and up to 3 researchers nominated by each institute.

**INTERAS** is part of **Group 8**, working in close cooperation with the **INCT for Molecular Sciences (CiMol)** and the **INCT for Gene Therapy (INTERGEN)**.

The first cycle of the program runs for 12 months (September 2026 to June 2027) and works as a thematic rotation. The activities are led by a team of 10 expert facilitators from Embrapii Units and Competence Centers in different regions of the country.

For each topic in the program, activities take place in three-week cycles (with a minimum of 20 hours), including:

1. **Initial assessment:** survey of the group's maturity stage and needs;
2. **Live virtual meetings:** conceptual discussions and alignment of practical methodologies;
3. **Guided practical application:** preparation of institutional plans, tools and models;
4. **Weekly Q&A sessions:** dedicated sessions for ongoing support to participants;
5. **Assessment and consolidation:** synthesis intervals for the continuous improvement of processes.

All interaction, schedule management and sharing of documents take place on the integrated platform of the Knowledge Journey Community.

### Thematic Training Axes

Over the twelve months of mentoring, the researchers will go through ten modules focused on institutional structuring and interaction with the business sector:

* **Management of R&D&I Projects with Companies:** technology horizon models, project selection and definition of key performance indicators (KPIs);
* **Preparing an R&D&I Project Plan:** formal structuring of collaborative proposals;
* **Recording R&D&I Deliverables:** methodologies for monitoring and validating technical deliverables;
* **Structuring Internal Processes:** legal, administrative and operational governance of research laboratories;
* **Prospecting Projects with Industry:** identifying and attracting demands from the productive sector;
* **Technology Transfer and Contracting:** best practices in licensing, contract models and project continuity;
* **Intellectual Property Management and Negotiation:** protecting and adding value to intellectual assets in public-private partnerships;
* **Communication Strategies:** science communication aimed at engaging companies and society;
* **Relationship with Support Foundations:** efficient compliance and budget execution;
* **Fundraising Strategy:** diversifying complementary sources of funding.

### INTERAS's Participation

To represent INCT INTERAS and share the knowledge gained with its laboratories and associated networks, three members of the institute's research committee were nominated:

* [Prof. Dr. Emiliano Medei](/equipe/emiliano-horacio-medei/) (INTERAS Vice-Coordinator and Full Professor at UFRJ);
* [Prof. Dr. Orlando Rodrigues Junior](/equipe/orlando-rodrigues-junior/) (Senior Researcher at IPEN-CNEN);
* [Prof. Dr. Mário Olímpio de Menezes](/equipe/mario-olimpio-de-menezes/) (Researcher at IPEN-CNEN).

The program's official calendar opened on **August 28, 2026** with the national *kickoff* meeting, at which the facilitators and the program guidelines were presented by the Embrapii and CNPq boards. The rounds of activities with the INCTs began on **September 1, 2026**.

In addition to the virtual cycles, the schedule includes three hybrid/in-person thematic workshops (planned for December 2026, March 2027 and May 2027), culminating in the closing seminar of the first cycle on **June 22, 2027**.

### Strengthening Innovation in Radiation in Health

INTERAS's participation in the Knowledge Journey is a strategic step towards consolidating the translation of the scientific knowledge generated at the institute into solutions applicable to public health and the medical industry.

Training in R&D&I management and industrial prospecting will strengthen the INTERAS ecosystem in the development of new radiopharmaceuticals, dosimetry instrumentation, biomedical technologies and therapeutic nanosystems, broadening the sustainability and social reach of publicly funded research.
