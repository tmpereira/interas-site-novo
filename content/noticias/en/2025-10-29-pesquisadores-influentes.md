The National Institute of Science and Technology for Radiation in Health (INTERAS) is immensely proud to celebrate the recognition of its researchers in the latest edition of the prestigious ranking of the world's most influential scientists, published by Elsevier and prepared by researchers at Stanford University.

The list, which assesses the impact of the scientific publications of researchers in all fields of knowledge, highlights the top 2% most cited scientists worldwide. Having INTERAS members on this select list is a testament to the excellence and relevance of the research carried out at our institute.

The presence of our researchers in this international ranking attests to the high-impact work carried out within INTERAS, focused on advancing knowledge about radiation and its applications in health, and contributes significantly to scientific progress and the well-being of society.

### A Collective Effort for Science

The inclusion of our members among the world's most influential scientists is a source of great joy and inspiration for all the researchers, students and technicians who make up INTERAS. We understand that science is a collective effort and that every advance, every publication and every successful project is the result of the collaboration and dedication of many.

We celebrate this important milestone for our researchers and for INTERAS, confident that the dedication of each of our members is essential to fulfilling our mission of generating frontier knowledge and developing technologies that improve people's quality of life.

The full list, published by Elsevier, is based on standardized citation metrics that assess the impact of a researcher's work over their career and in recent years. For more information on the methodology, see the [data published by Elsevier](https://elsevier.digitalcommonsdata.com/datasets/btchxktzyw/8).
