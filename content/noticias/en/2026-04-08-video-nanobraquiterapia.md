INTERAS has just released a new audiovisual production detailing frontier research in **Nanobrachytherapy**.

The video highlights the work of PhD student **Noé Gabriel P. Machado**, supervised by **Prof. Dr. Wagner de Rossi**, focusing on the synthesis of gold nanoparticles by laser ablation. This project is a practical example of the INTERAS mission: to integrate cutting-edge physics with direct applications in public health.

The material is part of the set of institutional videos that document the INCT's progress for our activity report.
