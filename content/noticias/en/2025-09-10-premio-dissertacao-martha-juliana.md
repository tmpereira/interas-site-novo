Congratulations to Dr. Martha Simões Ribeiro and student Juliana Cristina Oliverio de Araújo on the award for best dissertation in the Professional Master's (Stricto Sensu Graduate Studies) category.

Juliana's dissertation was entitled: **Fluorescence diagnosis and photodynamic treatment of actinic cheilitis**.

The work was carried out at IPEN, in the Professional Graduate Program - Professional Master's in Radiation Technology in Health Sciences.
