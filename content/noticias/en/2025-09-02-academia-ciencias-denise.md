---
titulo: INTERAS Coordinator elected to the São Paulo State Academy of Sciences
categorias: [outreach]
---

We are honored and delighted to announce that **Dr. Denise Zezell**, Coordinator of INTERAS, has been elected a Full Member of the São Paulo State Academy of Sciences (ACIESP) in the area of Physical Sciences, becoming a permanent member of the academy.

Nineteen outstanding scientists were elected, across ACIESP's ten areas. Of the 19 new members, 10 are women, about 53% of the total, a historic milestone for ACIESP.

The full story is on the [ACIESP website:](https://aciesp.org.br/novos-membros-da-aciesp-eleicao-2025/)
